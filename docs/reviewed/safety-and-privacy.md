# Safety and Privacy

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

Gander has Community Guidelines against harassment, threats, hate speech, illegal material and other harmful conduct. Posts and accounts can be reported. Gander says important moderation decisions involve human review. If a moderator removes content, the account owner receives an email explaining the reason; the appeal route is to reply to that email.

Version 1.1.1 added muted words and tags and stronger sensitive-content protection. Version 1.0.9 reported stronger two-way block enforcement. The location of current mute, block and reporting controls still needs an app check.

Human Check is one safety measure. Gander says it does not see your ID through Canada Post Identity+ or your banking details through the Interac option. Each method has its own access and privacy trade-offs. See [Human Check](human-check.md) for the current options.

Gander's privacy policy and App Store privacy disclosure are the right sources for data handling details. Read them before making a personal decision about what to share.

**Sources:** [Community Guidelines](https://gandersocial.ca/community-guidelines-2/), [Gander FAQ](https://gandersocial.ca/faq/), [October 30 Human Check update](https://gandersocial.ca/a-toonie-to-confirm-youre-human/), [App Store version history and privacy disclosure](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/gander-social/id6754884022).

**Check in app before adding:** current report, mute, block, sensitive-content and privacy screens.
