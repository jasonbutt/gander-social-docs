# Groups and News

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

Groups have arrived. Version 1.3.0 says members can create or join public and private communities, post as a group, and use group moderation tools. Version 1.3.1 adds context to posts seen outside a group by showing which group they came from. Those release notes supersede the older FAQ's statement that Groups are still being built.

The News feed also arrived in version 1.3.0. Gander describes it as a feed of recent stories from trusted news sources. News links can be shared in ordinary posts too. Gander says it labels some news links with media bias and factual reporting information from Media Bias/Fact Check. A label is context for the reader, not a substitute for reading and checking the source.

Public and private group boundaries, moderator powers, News feed selection and current screen labels need an in-app review. This page keeps to confirmed release claims until then.

**Sources:** [App Store version 1.3.0 and 1.3.1 history](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/gander-social/id6754884022), [Gander FAQ](https://gandersocial.ca/faq/), [How Gander works](https://gandersocial.ca/how-it-works/).

**Check in app before adding:** group creation and privacy flow, moderation controls, News feed filters.
