# Human Check

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

Human Check is Gander's participation gate. You can create an account, read public posts and follow accounts without it. Gander asks for confirmation when you want to post, comment or share. The official FAQ also says chatting needs Human Check.

Canada Post Identity+ checks identity without sending Gander your ID or the personal details on it. Gander's FAQ also lists an eligible institutional email option. The newer option uses a **$2 Interac Money Request** initiated on Gander and completed through your online banking. Gander says it receives confirmation that the transaction went through, not your banking details. The full $2 goes to Food Banks Canada. This option requires online banking and two dollars; it is not accessible to everyone. The FAQ has not yet been updated for the Interac option, so check the current Human Check screen for the methods offered to your account.

Gander describes Human Check as a useful signal that an account belongs to an adult human. It is not a guarantee and does not replace moderation. Pseudonyms remain welcome. The available methods currently limit active participation to people in Canada; Gander plans to expand access.

If a method fails, use the current Human Check screen or Gander support guidance. The exact screen sequence has not been verified here.

**Sources:** [October 30 Human Check update](https://gandersocial.ca/a-toonie-to-confirm-youre-human/), [Gander FAQ](https://gandersocial.ca/faq/), [App Store version 1.3.1 history](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/gander-social/id6754884022).
