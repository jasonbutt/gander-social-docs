# Boards

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

Boards let you save and arrange favourite posts around a topic. The App Store description says you can pin posts in custom boards and share the collection. A board might hold recipes, reading recommendations or a particularly fine run of #PetTax. It is a small library for the things you want to find again.

The old community guide contains button-by-button Board instructions and screenshots from an earlier build. They have been removed from this transfer set because the current screens have not been checked. Board visibility, collaboration and cross-app behaviour need confirmation in the app before we describe them as settled.

Gander's FAQ cautions that platform-specific features such as Boards may not appear or behave the same way in other AT Protocol apps.

**Sources:** [Canadian App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/gander-social/id6754884022), [Gander FAQ](https://gandersocial.ca/faq/), [How Gander works](https://gandersocial.ca/how-it-works/).

**Check in app before adding:** Board creation, saving, visibility, sharing and deletion.
