# Feeds and Nest

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

Think of the feeds as different flight paths. Gander says most feeds are chronological. The Active Conversations area of For You is an exception: it highlights discussions involving you or people you follow. Gander's For You and topic feeds offer other ways to discover people and posts.

Your Nest is the inner circle of accounts you do not want to miss. Gander describes it as a focused feed for people who matter to you. Its May release notes say an account can be added to the Nest from a post or profile. Version 1.3.1 says the Nest feed now loads more content as you scroll.

Gander has described a network choice between Gander-only and Gander plus Bluesky. The current location and wording of that control need checking in the app. Feed labels and ranking may change, so this page avoids a fixed tap sequence.

**Sources:** [Gander FAQ](https://gandersocial.ca/faq/), [How Gander works](https://gandersocial.ca/how-it-works/), [May 2026 release notes](https://gandersocial.ca/gander-release-v1-0-2-may-8-2026/), [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/gander-social/id6754884022).

**Check in app before adding:** current feed names, scope controls, and Nest management screens.
