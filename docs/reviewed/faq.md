# FAQ

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

## Can I join from outside Canada?

Yes. Gander says anyone can sign up, browse and follow. The current Human Check methods are available only in Canada, so active participation is limited for now.

## Do I need Human Check to read?

No. You can read public posts and follow accounts without it. Posting, commenting and chatting require it.

## What can I share?

Text, images and galleries, videos, links and Flicks are listed as current formats. Stories, Events and Buy & Sell are described as work in progress in the official FAQ, so check a current release before assuming they have arrived.

## Are Groups available?

Yes. The October version 1.3.0 notes announced public and private Groups. The older FAQ has not caught up on this point.

## Can I edit a post?

Gander's FAQ says no. You can delete a post.

## Does every Gander feature work in Bluesky?

No guarantee. Shared AT Protocol records can interoperate, while Boards, Groups and other platform-specific features may behave differently or be absent.
