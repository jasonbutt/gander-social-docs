# Updates and Tips

*Unofficial community intelligence. Last reviewed: 2026-11-01.*

## Recent flight log

- **Version 1.3.1:** Group posts show their originating group in other feeds. Human Check gained a $2 Interac option whose full amount goes to Food Banks Canada. The release also mentions clearer errors and smoother Groups and Nest scrolling.
- **Version 1.3.0:** Public and private Groups and a News feed arrived. Video and photo posting received upload improvements.
- **Version 1.1.1:** Dark mode, muted words and tags, and stronger sensitive-content protection arrived.

These are release notes, not a promise that every account sees the same control in the same place. Check the current app before writing instructions about a screen.

## Tips from the flock

Add context when sharing a link. Use descriptive text for images when the current composer supports it. Write multiword hashtags with capital letters so they are easier to read. Curate a Nest for accounts you want to keep up with. Save useful posts to a Board so they do not disappear down the timeline.

See a signal drift? [Open an issue](https://github.com/jasonbutt/gander-social-docs/issues/new/choose) or propose a Markdown change. Approved edits are transferred into Offprint and synced to the canonical guide record.
